# SpinFlow 0.4.0b2 development status

**0.4.0b2 remains an unreleased experimental candidate.** The current public release is [0.4.0b1](https://github.com/ben-shin/spinflow/releases/tag/v0.4.0b1); its published release and guide are unchanged. This status covers source commit [`9c09e89`](https://github.com/ben-shin/spinflow/commit/9c09e89ae1d2928097640f8c55451c6916ed5b53).

The commit’s [Ubuntu validation run](https://github.com/ben-shin/spinflow/actions/runs/43348788727) reports five successful jobs, and its [release-build run](https://github.com/ben-shin/spinflow/actions/runs/43348956935) reports three. A separate installed-wheel suite on Ubuntu 24.04 x86-64 with CPython 3.12 passed 1,280 ordinary tests and 101 subtests; 16 explicitly designated cache-gated cases were not executed, with zero failures or errors. The installed parent wheel’s identity was verified; some CLI child processes intentionally used source-forced imports. The current-source regression report includes the previously unavailable cache-accounting and test-origin paths, but skipped cache-gated cases are not covered by that pass. The CPython 3.13 CI result is installer and GUI/plot smoke coverage, not a second full installed-suite result.

This revision updates **cache retained-byte accounting** for eligible immutable borrowed NMRPipe storage and fixes the source-origin test setup. These checks establish the declared behaviors for the tested profile; they are not a matched performance benchmark or a claim about total process memory. Six release assets were assembled. Separately, comparison of the 31 packaged product files found `_identity.py` changed; the other 30 packaged product files, including the native binary, are byte-identical. The package version remains 0.4.0b2. The candidate is not published.

An earlier 0.4.0b2 revision had an incomplete suite and a cache-accounting finding; those remain historical results for that revision. They do not describe the current installed-suite outcome. No new-revision full original acquired-method or end-user-application acceptance is claimed.

Scientific and researcher acceptance remain open. The six-of-18 fixed-target qualification failure remains in force. Full original NMRPipe protein processing, nonzero phase and LP/POLY compatibility, broader vendor/profile coverage, independent end-user reopening, and physical researcher acceptance have not been established. No stable, v1, general parity, or clinical-use claim is made.
